Detailed Description

A class for a matching information between channels of two branches (id1 of branch1, id2 of branch2).

All pairs are stored in CSR layout: the partners of id1 are ids2_[offsets_[id1], offsets_[id1 + 1]), in the order they were added. By convention the best partner is added first, GetMatch() returns it. The inverse direction (id2 -> id1) is not persisted, it is built on first use and ordered by id1. Ids are channel ids, i.e. indices in the Detector, and must be non-negative.
